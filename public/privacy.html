EYEWALL ANALYTICS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

EyeWall Analytics ("EyeWall", "we", "us") provides NHL and PWHL advanced stats and analytics through a website and Android app. This page explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

CategoryWhatWhy
App usage analytics Anonymous device ID, pages/tabs viewed, features used (e.g. which team or game stats you open), general device/browser info Understand which features are useful, fix bugs, improve the app. Collected via PostHog. Not tied to your name or email — you are not signed in for this.
Account & sign-in Email address, sign-in session Optional passwordless sign-in (magic link). Only collected if you choose to sign in.
Saved preferences (signed-in users) Favorite team/sport, trivia answer history So your picks and trivia progress sync across your devices.
Saved preferences (all users) Favorite team, theme, notification settings — stored locally on your device only Remember your settings between visits. Never leaves your device unless you're signed in.
Push notifications A push subscription address issued by your browser/device, plus which team and alert types you subscribed to (goals, penalties, final scores) Deliver the game alerts you opted into. Only collected if you enable notifications; not linked to your name or email.

We do not collect payment information, and EyeWall has no in-app purchases or paid subscriptions.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • We do not share your email address with analytics or advertising providers.

Third-party services

We use the following processors to operate the app:

  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics. See PostHog's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — authentication and data storage for signed-in features. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts, served from Google's infrastructure.

Your choices

  • You can use EyeWall fully without signing in or enabling notifications — team/theme preferences still work, stored only on your device.
  • You can disable push notifications at any time from the app's notification settings, or from your device's system settings.
  • To delete your account and associated data (email, saved preferences, trivia history), email matt@eyewallanalytics.com.

Children's privacy

EyeWall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
