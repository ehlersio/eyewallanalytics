Delete Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your EyeWall Analytics account and all data associated with it at any time.

Send a deletion request from the email address on your account to:

matt@eyewallanalytics.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Saved preferences (favorite team, favorite sport)
  • Trivia answer history
  • Any active push notification subscriptions tied to your account

Turnaround time

Requests are processed within 30 days. You'll get a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Data that isn't tied to an account

Anonymous product-analytics events (page views, feature usage) aren't linked to your email or identity, so they can't be looked up or deleted individually — see the Privacy Policy for details on what's collected.
